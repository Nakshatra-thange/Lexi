MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between
Vendor Solutions Inc. ("Vendor") and Customer Corp ("Customer").

1. SERVICES
Vendor shall provide software development and consulting services as
described in each Statement of Work executed under this Agreement.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT
Customer shall pay Vendor's fees within thirty (30) days of invoice.
Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
IN NO EVENT SHALL VENDOR'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED
THE AMOUNT OF FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE ONE (1) MONTH PRECEDING THE
CLAIM. VENDOR SHALL NOT BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, OR
CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES.

4. INDEMNIFICATION
Customer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Vendor from and
against any and all third-party claims, damages, or expenses arising
out of Customer's use of the deliverables. Vendor makes no
indemnification obligations to Customer under this Agreement.

5. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial term of three (3)
years. Either party may terminate this Agreement only for material
breach that remains uncured for sixty (60) days following written notice.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All deliverables, work product, source code, and other materials
developed by Vendor in connection with this Agreement shall remain the
sole and exclusive property of Vendor. Customer is granted a
non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the deliverables solely
for Customer's internal business purposes during the term of this
Agreement.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party agrees to keep the other party's Confidential Information
confidential and not disclose it to third parties without prior written
consent. This obligation shall terminate upon expiration or termination
of this Agreement.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Cayman Islands,
without regard to conflict of law principles. Any disputes shall be
resolved exclusively in the courts of the Cayman Islands.

9. RENEWAL
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year
terms unless Customer provides written notice of non-renewal at least
five (5) business days prior to the end of the then-current term.

10. WARRANTY
Vendor provides the deliverables "as is" with no warranties of any
kind, express or implied, including but not limited to warranties of
merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

11. MISCELLANEOUS
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
